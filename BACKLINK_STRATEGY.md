## 3. Broken Link Building

**The strategy:** Find marketing blog posts or resource pages that link to tools that no longer exist, have been acquired, or have broken pages. Email the author and suggest freewebaudits.com as a replacement.

**How to find opportunities:**

1. **Find old influencer marketing tool lists** — Search queries:
   - `"best influencer marketing tools 2019"` — many tools from 2019 are now defunct
   - `"free website auditor tools"` — old lists have broken links
   - `"fake follower checker tools"` — this niche had many tools shut down
   - `"influencer verification tools"` — lots of 2020-era startups that folded

2. **Specific defunct/broken tools to look for:**
   - **HypeAuditor** — still active but changed pricing (many free list posts link to it)
   - **SocialAuditor.io** — appears in older lists (verify if still active)
   - **Modash** (modash.io) — paid-only now; free lists may link here
   - ** influencer.co** — defunct
   - ** ** — various 2019-era influencer tools that shut down
   - ** — Checkatron and similar legacy tools
   - **NinjaOutreach** — pivoted/closed
   - **Buzzstream** (influencer outreach features) — older guides reference it
   - **Right Relevance** — defunct influencer scoring tool

3. **Search for broken resource pages to target:**
   ```
   site:blog.hubspot.com "influencer tools" -2024
   site:blog.bufferapp.com "free tools" "influencer"
   site:coschedule.com "influencer" "tools" "2019"
   site:socialmediaexaminer.com "influencer marketing tools" "2020"
   ```

4. **Find resource pages using Ahrefs/Semrush (when you have access):**
   - Search for pages ranking for "best free marketing tools" or "free website auditor"
   - Check their outbound links for 404s using: `site:ahrefs.com "site:example.com" broken links`

### Specific Broken Link Opportunities to Pursue

**Opportunity 1: Old "Top 10 Free Marketing Tools" blog posts**
- Many blogs published these in 2019-2022 listing tools that are now paid or defunct
- Tools to look for: Google Analytics Alternatives, old Screaming Frog competitors, defunct SEO tools
- **Pitch:** Email the author: "I noticed you link to [defunct tool] in your [post name] but it appears to be down. I run freewebaudits.com — a free website auditing tool that does [X]. Would you consider updating the link? Happy to write a brief blurb for you."

**Opportunity 2: Influencer marketing "tools comparison" posts from 2020-2021**
- Many startups from that era are gone. Check: Followerwonk (Moz), Klout (defunct), Kred (defunct), Twitter Grader (defunct)
- **Pitch:** "I noticed your post references Klout/Kred/[defunct tool]. If you're open to updating it, freewebaudits.com offers a free website audit and influencer credibility check. No pitch, just a suggestion."

**Opportunity 3: Link to tool pages that no longer exist (404s)**
- When a tool rebrands or is acquired, their marketing pages often 404
- Example: Page redirects to a pricing page that doesn't exist anymore

## 4. HARO / Help A Reporter Out

### What is HARO?

HARO (Help A Reporter Out) connects journalists with expert sources. Sign up as a **Source** (you provide quotes) and get quoted in articles on major publications — with a link back to your website.

**Website:** https://www.helpareporter.com/
**Cost:** Free for sources (paid plans available for PR professionals)
**Alternative/Qfrench:** qfrench.com — similar service, worth signing up for both

### How to Sign Up

1. Go to https://www.helpareporter.com/
2. Click **"I'm a Source"** → Create free account
3. Set your expertise categories: select *"Marketing"*, *"Technology"*, *"Social Media"*, *"Entrepreneurs"*, *"Small Business"*, *"Consumer Products"*
4. Set email alert frequency: **Daily** (morning and evening digest)
5. Reply to relevant queries within 2-4 hours — speed matters

### What Queries to Look For

HARO sends three daily emails. Watch for queries containing:

- "influencer marketing" / "fake followers" / "influencer fraud"
- "marketing technology" / "marketing tools"
- "website auditing" / "SEO tools"
- "social media marketing" / "Instagram marketing"
- "B2B marketing" / "content marketing tools"
- "brand safety" / "ad fraud" / "marketing fraud"
- "startup marketing" / "growth hacking"

### Example HARO Queries to Respond To

When a journalist asks about *"best free marketing tools for startups"*, reply with:
> "LeadAudit Pro (freewebaudits.com) is a free website auditing tool that helps marketers identify technical SEO issues, check site health, and verify the authenticity of influencer partners before campaign spend. It's particularly useful for teams that need quick credibility checks on potential influencer partners."

When asked about *"how to detect fake influencers"*:
> "The most reliable indicators are engagement ratio anomalies, follower growth velocity, and cross-platform consistency. Tools like LeadAudit Pro allow you to run a full website and social credibility audit of an influencer's digital presence in minutes. The key metrics to check: engagement rate vs follower count, URL domain authority if they link from their blog, and whether their website has been audited."

### HARO Response Template

```
Subject: Expert source available — re: [query subject line]

Hi [Journalist name],

I'm available to provide a quote on this topic.

[Your quote — 2-3 sentences, specific, data-backed if possible]

My background: [Name] is [title] at [Company]. [One sentence about why you're qualified to speak on this].

Happy to provide more detail or speak further if helpful.

Best,
[Your name]
[LinkedIn profile URL]
[Company website: freewebaudits.com]
```

## What NOT to Do

These tactics will hurt freewebaudits.com more than help:

- ❌ **Buying links** — violates Google guidelines, risk of manual penalty
- ❌ **PBN networks** — private blog networks are detectable and penalized
- ❌ **Comment spam** — "Great post!" with a link on unrelated blogs = no value + spam flag
- ❌ **Forum signature links** — links in forum profiles on unrelated forums
- ❌ **Link exchanges** — "you link to me, I'll link to you" — Google detects reciprocal patterns
- ❌ **Press release distribution services** — generic PR wire distribution with spammy backlinks
- ❌ **Auto-approved directory submissions** — any directory that approves everyone with no editorial review

**The rule:** Would this link make sense to the reader if there were no SEO benefit? If yes, it's a good link. If no, don't pursue it.
