Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?
One free audit per IP address per day, automatically. No sign-up, no credit card. Just paste a YouTube channel URL and get your audit instantly.
What does the authenticity score mean?
The score (0–100) is calculated using view-to-subscriber ratios, video engagement rates, and subscriber count plausibility. Above 80 = highly authentic. Below 40 = likely fake or inflated followers.
What platforms are supported?
Currently YouTube only. Instagram and TikTok support are in development. Subscribe to Pro to get early access when new platforms launch.
Is my audit data stored?
Audit reports are stored temporarily for download. We do not share your audits with third parties. Paid subscribers can request full data deletion at any time.
Can I use this for client work?
Yes. Pro accounts can use audits for client deliverables, agency work, and brand partnership decisions. Single reports are licensed for one-time use.
