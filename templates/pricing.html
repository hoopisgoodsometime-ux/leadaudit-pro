Common Questions

Is the free website audit really free?+
Yes — 100% free, no signup, no email required. You get a complete SEO, speed, SSL, and mobile audit instantly.
What does influencer audit actually check?+
We analyze subscriber count, total views, view-to-subscriber ratio, recent video engagement rates (likes/comments per view), and overall pattern consistency to estimate authenticity.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes. Monthly plans cancel anytime. Pay-per-use has no subscription — just buy reports as you need them.
How accurate is the fake follower detection?+
The ARVIA gene-traced scoring system cross-references multiple signals (engagement ratios, view patterns, ratio thresholds) to give a calibrated estimate. It's a strong signal, not a guarantee — always combine with qualitative judgment.
What's the calibration feedback loop?+
Every audit recommendation is tracked. When you confirm or refute a recommendation (did it actually work?), that signal feeds back into the ARVIA gene system — making future audits smarter over time.
